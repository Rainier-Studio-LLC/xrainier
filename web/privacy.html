XRainier Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Rainier Studio LLC, doing business as XRainier

The short version. We build apps and games that work without handing over more than they need. We collect as little personal information as each product needs to work, we never sell it, and we don't run ads or advertising trackers. Each product also has its own privacy policy with the details; this page covers what they have in common and the xrainier.com website.

Who we are

XRainier is a trademark of Rainier Studio LLC, an independent software company in the Seattle area, Washington, USA. Rainier Studio LLC is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: support@xrainier.com.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to xrainier.com and to every app and game published under the XRainier name. Each product also has its own privacy policy, linked from the Privacy link at the bottom of that product's pages, which explains exactly what that product collects and why. Where a product policy is more specific, it applies for that product.

The xrainier.com website

  • No account, no sign-in and no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Your browser keeps a small copy of our product list (which products are live) in its own storage, so the menu loads quickly. It holds no personal information.
  • The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives a normal web request from your browser (including your IP address) under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address to reply and keep them as long as needed to help you.

What our products may collect

Not every product does all of this; many do none of it. The product's own policy says which apply.

Things you give us

  • Account details, when a product offers an account: your email address, a display name you choose, and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the account identifier they give us.
  • Things you post in chat or community features, such as messages, photos or reports, so they can be delivered to the people you're talking to and kept safe from abuse.
  • Support requests you send us.

Things collected as you use a product

  • Location, only where a feature needs it (for example, showing what is in the sky above you) and only after your device asks your permission. We round or limit it wherever the feature allows.
  • Game data such as scores, progress and settings, and basic technical data such as device type and error reports needed to keep the product working.
  • Standard request data such as IP addresses, processed by our hosting provider to deliver the service and protect it from attacks.

Payments

Paid plans are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details; we never see or store your full card number. We receive what we need to run your plan, such as its status, renewal date and the email on the purchase.

How we use it

  • To run each product and the features you choose, including sign-in, sync, chat and paid plans.
  • To keep products safe and working: preventing abuse and cheating, fixing problems and handling reports.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to send account and purchase messages you need. We don't send marketing email unless you ask for it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't use your photos, messages or location to build profiles about you.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the products, under contracts that limit their use of it, such as hosting (Cloudflare), payments (Stripe), email delivery, and sign-in with Google or Apple when you choose them. We also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, with this policy continuing to apply.

Children

Most XRainier products are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. Products designed for children are built to work without collecting children's personal information, and their own policies explain how. We don't knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as the product needs it, then delete it. Chat features that promise messages disappear delete them on that schedule. When you delete your account, we delete its personal information, except what we must keep by law (for example, purchase records for tax).

Your choices and rights

  • You can see, correct, download or delete your information. Many products let you do this in their settings; otherwise email support@xrainier.com, and we will respond within 30 days.
  • You can turn off location in your device settings at any time; features that need it will stop working.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Washington, the EU or the UK), you may have further rights, such as to object to or limit some processing, or to complain to your data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using them.

Security and storage

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) for every product, end-to-end encryption where a product says so, and access controls on our systems. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you as the law requires. We are based in the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries, with safeguards required by law.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the affected products.
