Live radar

Every plane over your head

Open SkyHere and see what's flying above you right now, with a card for each flight sorted by distance.

  • Altitude, speed in knots and mph, heading and distance on every card
  • A LIVE badge and a refresh every few seconds
  • Opens instantly with your last scan when you come back
  • Star your favorite flights and airlines
Flight details

Where it's from, where it's going

Tap any flight for the full story: route, aircraft, photos and how the flight is going.

  • Origin and destination, airline and aircraft type
  • Aircraft photos, with a slideshow in Pro
  • Whole-flight altitude and speed history in Pro
  • Find any flight by number, call sign or registration in Pro
  • Share a live link anyone can follow
3D replay

Fly it again, in 3D

Replay a flight on a 2D map or in a full 3D globe, from outside or from the cockpit.

  • 2D and 3D views of the whole flight
  • Cockpit view with a heads-up display
  • 11 detailed aircraft models painted in airline colors
  • Full-fin airline tail art
Showcase

One flight, full screen

Showcase mode turns any screen into a calm flight display: one plane at a time, big and clear, with the time and date.

  • Great on a TV, a spare tablet or a second monitor
  • Cycles through the flights around you
  • Clean screen with no watermark in Pro
Spotters

You saw it. So did they.

Tap I saw it to join the people who spotted the same plane today, and earn badges as you go.

  • Reactions: 👍 nice, 😍 love it, 📸 got a photo, 🔊 loud
  • Nicknames with badges like Heavy hunter, Night owl and Globetrotter
  • Follow other spotters and see what they saw
  • World and local leaderboards, plus rare planes being watched near you
Sky Link

Same flight, anywhereNEW

When other spotters mark the same flight as you, near or far, SkyHere links you and shows it on a map. Then you can all talk about it.

  • On for every signed-in spotter, at any distance
  • 👥 Group chat with everyone who saw the flight, from your Sky Link
  • A notice in the app and on the website when it happens
  • A shareable page with the route, the plane live and every spotter's area
  • Areas are approximate: exact locations are never shared
Sky Link chat

Meet the spotters on your flightNEW

Tap I'm on this flight when you board, or open the group chat from a Sky Link when you spotted it from the ground. Everyone on the same flight chats together, and you can ask anyone there for a private chat.

  • Messages, photos, videos, drawings, voice notes and reactions
  • End-to-end encrypted: only the people in the chat can read, see or hear it. Not even SkyHere can
  • Gone in a day: flight chats 24 hours after landing, group chats 24 hours after the last sighting, private messages 24 hours after they're sent
  • Private chats open only when the other person accepts
  • Your spotter name and profile photo only: your email and exact location are never shown
  • Report and block built in; slow mode and link limits keep busy chats spam-free

How we protect chat is explained in the SkyHere privacy policy.

Sky Link chat

Speak your language. They read theirs.NEW

Live translation lets spotters from anywhere chat on the same flight. Speak or type in your language, and the translation appears as you go. Press send and they get it in theirs.

  • Translates while you speak or type: no extra taps, check it before you send
  • 21 languages, including English, 日本語, 普通话, 繁體中文, 粵語 Cantonese, 한국어, Español, Français and Deutsch
  • Speak to type in any of them, then send in another, like speaking Cantonese and sending Japanese
  • Messages you receive can be translated into your language too, with the original one tap away
  • Prefer your own words? Send original anytime
