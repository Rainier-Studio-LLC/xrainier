Inside Derekly

Everything for the trip, nothing in the way

Know where you stand

The overview shows a countdown, a “ready” score, and flights, stays, car and tickets as booked, pending or missing, with a list of what still needs doing.

Paste a confirmation

Paste a booking email or attach the ticket. Derekly reads the dates, times, flight numbers, confirmation code and price, in seven languages.

Plan with AI

Tell it who’s going and where. Get the best things to see, do, stay, eat, and events on those dates for your kids’ ages, then pick what you like.

A map for every day

Each day is a timeline beside a map, with numbered stops, the driving route and the distance between them. One tap opens the whole day in Google Maps.

Weather and daylight

Temperatures for every day (a forecast when it’s close, last year’s otherwise), plus sunrise, sunset and hours of daylight. Handy for aurora trips.

Money at a glance

A ring chart by category (flights, stays, car, tickets, food), with budget used, what’s paid and what’s left. Tap a category to see its costs.

Checklist and packing

To-dos with due dates and a packing list. Overdue items show up in “Needs attention” before they become a problem.

Share, print, calendar

Send family a view-only link with costs and notes hidden, print a clean itinerary or save it as a PDF, or add every stop to your calendar.

Seven languages, any device

English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, 日本語, Français and Deutsch on phone, tablet and computer. Works offline and syncs when you sign in.

Plan with AI

Tell it who’s going. Pick what you love.

  • Your family first: first and last names, and the children’s ages.
  • Where and when: the destination, your dates and what you enjoy: nature, museums, food, shows and more.
  • Ideas, made for you: things to see and do, places to stay and eat, and events on those dates, each with why it suits your kids, best ages, time and cost.
  • A plan in seconds: your picks become a relaxed day-by-day trip with bookings and a checklist. Change anything afterwards.

Prefer your own notes? Paste a written plan (an email, a doc, in any language) and Derekly turns it into a trip.

Day by day

A map for every day, a rhythm for the family

  • A timeline of the day next to a big map, with the numbered stops and the road route between them.
  • Distance between stops, so the day isn’t too long for little ones.
  • The day’s weather, sunrise, sunset and hours of daylight, worked out for any date.
  • One tap opens the whole day in Google Maps for the drive.

Private by design

Your trips stay yours

No ads, no trackers

Without an account, your trips never leave your device. With one, they sync across your devices and nothing more. We never sell your data.

City level, at most

We don’t use GPS or keep IP addresses. AI gets only what you ask it about, and only children’s ages, never their names.

Yours to take or delete

Export any trip as a file, a calendar or a PDF. Delete your account and everything in it from Settings, at any time.
