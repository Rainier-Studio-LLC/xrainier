How it plays

Spin it. Aim it. Drop it.

Flat block puzzles let you place a piece. BlockRush 3D lets you turn it over in your hand first.

Spin

Drag the piece in the tray to turn it any way you like, a full 360° on every axis. Let go and it clicks into the nearest square position.

QE turn WS tilt AD roll

Aim

Drag over the well and a see-through ghost shows exactly where the piece will land. Drag outside the well to look around.

←→↑↓ aim V view

Drop

Tap the same spot again, press Drop or click. Complete a line of five in any direction and it vanishes. Can't see inside? Switch on X-ray.

Space drop X x-ray
