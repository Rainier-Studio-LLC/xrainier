How it plays

Easy to pick up, wild to master

Arcade handling on top of real physics. Every boost is earned on the track, never bought.

Drift → boost

Hold a drift through a corner to charge sparks, then release for a burst of speed. The longer the drift, the bigger the boost.

BLUEORANGEPURPLE

Slipstream

Sit right behind a rival for a moment and the draft slingshots you past. Leading is never safe.

Jumps

Hills turn into ramps. Hit the crest straight and you fly; land crooked and you lose the line.

No weapons

No shells, no bananas, no paid speed. Every car has the same top speed, so the best line wins.

A track code is a whole circuit: world, length, twist, hills and a seed. The same code builds the same track on every device, so you share tracks as text. Paste one in a chat and your friends are on the grid.

FAQ

Questions

What do I need to play?

A modern browser with WebGL2: current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to download or install. Open turbotoons.xrainier.com and race.

Do I need an account?

No. You race as a guest, and pick a name only if you want to show up on the leaderboards. No email needed.

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free gets you online Quick Race, Track of the Day with its leaderboard, Ghost Duels, the Track Maker and three cars: Pip, Comet and Bruno. TurboToons Pro adds all 8 cars, 12 paints and decals, private lobbies up to 12, unlimited saved tracks, any top-50 ghost and the replay theater for $14.99 a year or $2.49 a month.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, anytime. Pro starts with a 7-day free trial, and payments are handled by Stripe.

Is Pro pay-to-win?

No. Every car shares one top speed and boosts only come from drifting and slipstreams. Pro is cosmetics, private lobbies and creator conveniences.

How does online racing work?

Racers connect directly to each other, peer-to-peer, which keeps lag low. As in any WebRTC game, the other racers in your lobby can see your IP address. See the TurboToons privacy policy.
