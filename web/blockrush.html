How it plays

Easy to learn, hard to put down

You get a hand of three pieces from 32 shapes. Place all three to get a new hand. The run ends when nothing in your hand fits, so every drop counts.

Drag & drop

Drag a piece onto the board with your finger or mouse. A ghost preview shows exactly where it lands.

Clear lines

Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Lines light up before you drop, so you can see what's about to go.

Combo streaks

Clear on move after move and your streak multiplies every clear. It survives up to two quiet placements, so plan ahead.

Clean sweep

Empty the whole board for a +300 bonus. Rare, loud and very satisfying.

Fair play

Every score is replayed. Every one.

Your browser never sends a score. It sends your moves, and the BlockRush server replays them from the same pieces with the same game engine. Only the score it computes goes on the board.

FAQ

Questions

What do I need to play?

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to download or install. Open blockrush.xrainier.com and play.

Is BlockRush free?

Yes. BlockRush is free to play: the Daily, Endless, Duels and the leaderboards are all included, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. You play as a guest and only pick a name to show up on the leaderboards. No email needed.

When does a new Daily start?

The Daily puzzle changes with the date at midnight UTC. Everyone gets the same pieces, and only your first finished run counts on the Daily board.

How do you stop cheating?

The game sends your moves, never a score. The server replays every run with the same engine and only the score it computes is ranked. Illegal moves and unfinished runs are rejected.

What if I close the tab mid-game?

Your run is saved on your device after every move, so you can come back and carry on.
