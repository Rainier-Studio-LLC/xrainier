How it plays

Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

Three pieces at a time, one board, no timer. A run takes a few minutes, and every move matters.

  1. 1

    Drag a piece

    Pick any of the three pieces and drop it on the board. A ghost shows where it lands and lights up the lines it will finish.

  2. 2

    Clear lines

    Fill a row or a column and it clears. Finish several at once for a big bonus. Place all three pieces to get a new hand.

  3. 3

    Chain the combo

    Keep clearing on consecutive moves to grow your combo multiplier. It survives two quiet placements, so plan ahead. The run ends when no piece fits.

Scoring cells placed + 10 × lines² × combo Clear the whole board for a +300 bonus. Two lines at once is worth 4× one line; a ×5 combo multiplies it again.

Fair play

Every score is proven.

Your browser never sends a score. It sends your moves, and our server replays the whole run with the same game engine to work out the score itself.

  • Replayed move by move. Impossible placements, edited scores and unfinished runs are rejected.
  • One shot at the Daily. Only your first finished run counts, for everyone.
  • No account needed. Pick a nickname and play. No email, no password.
  • Moderated boards. Suspicious runs can be re-verified and hidden.

FAQ

Questions

Is BlockRush free?

Yes. Play it free in any modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download and no account to create.

When does the Daily change?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same pieces in the same order, and only your first finished run counts on the board.

How is the score counted?

You get a point for every cell you place, plus 10 × lines² × your combo for every move that clears lines, plus 300 for clearing the whole board. The combo grows with each clearing move and survives two placements that clear nothing.

How do duels work?

After an endless run, tap Challenge a friend to get a link. Your friend plays the exact same pieces in the same order and sees your score to beat.

Can people cheat the leaderboard?

Your browser sends your moves, not your score. The server replays every move with the same game engine and only ranks runs that check out.

What do you keep about me?

Just the nickname you pick, your runs and a rough country for the boards. No email, no password.
