Listening

01No sound when I tap Listen

Try these in order:

  1. Opened from a chat or social app? Kavodly links opened inside LINE, WeChat, WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook often can’t speak. Tap ••• or the share button and choose Open in Safari (or Open in browser).
  2. Turn the volume up with the side buttons while it is reading. Media volume can be at zero even when the ringer is loud.
  3. iPhone silent switch or Action button: turn silent mode off once, then try again.
  4. No voice for this language: add one (see the next question).
  5. Still silent? Close the browser completely (swipe it away) and open Kavodly again. iPhone speech sometimes gets stuck.
  6. Try ✦ Natural voice in 🎙 Voice. It doesn’t use your phone’s voices.

Kavodly shows a “No sound?” card with these steps if nothing plays after a few seconds.

02My phone has fewer voices than my computer

Read-aloud voices come from your device, not from Kavodly, so each phone, computer and browser has its own list. Microsoft Edge on a PC adds many online voices; an iPhone starts with only one or two per language.

iPhone / iPad: On iPhone and iPad, Apple lets websites use only the voices that come with the phone. Voices you download in Settings work in Apple's own apps, but not on web pages, in any browser. That is why you see only a few voices here.

For the best voice on iPhone, choose ✦ Natural voice: it sounds like a person reading and is the same on every device.

Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output → tap ⚙ next to the engine → Install voice data.
Mac: In Chrome or Edge: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices. (Safari shows only the built-in voices.)
Windows: Microsoft Edge already includes many online voices. Or: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices.

03Natural voice is busy or missing

✦ Natural voice is made on our server, so it sounds the same on every device and needs an internet connection. It’s available in English, Chinese (both scripts), Spanish, French and Japanese; German uses your device’s voices for now.

If it’s busy, Kavodly switches to your device’s voice so reading continues. Tap 🎙 Voice and choose ✦ Natural voice again later.

04Reading stops when the screen locks or I switch apps

Phones pause speech from web pages in the background to save battery. Keep Kavodly open on screen while it reads. To stop the screen locking, set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock to a longer time while you listen.

Turn on Keep reading the next chapter in 🎙 Voice to go on to the next chapter by itself.

05Background music doesn’t play
  1. Open Aa (Reading settings) and turn on Background music.
  2. Tap anywhere on the page once. Browsers only start sound after a tap.
  3. Turn the volume up while it plays.

Sign-in & account

06My sign-in code email didn’t arrive
  1. Wait a minute, then check Junk / Spam (and Promotions in Gmail).
  2. The email comes from no-reply@xrainier.com. Add it to your contacts so it isn’t filtered.
  3. Check the address you typed. Tap Use another email to fix it.
  4. A code works for 15 minutes. If it has expired, ask for a new one. Only the newest code works.

You can also tap the Sign in button in the email instead of typing the code.

07“Continue with Google” doesn’t work

Google blocks sign-in inside other apps’ built-in browsers (LINE, WeChat, Instagram, Facebook…). Open Kavodly in Safari or Chrome and try again.

If a window opens and closes, allow pop-ups for kavodly.xrainier.com. Or simply use Email me a code; it signs you in to the same account if the email address is the same.

08I signed in on a phone I no longer have

Open the account button (👤) → Signed-in devices. Tap Sign out next to that device, or Sign out all other devices.

Notes & sync

09My notes don’t show on my other device
  1. Sign in with the same account on both devices (same email, or the same Google account).
  2. On the new device, open 👤 Account and unlock sync with the sync passcode you chose the first time.
  3. Tap Sync now on both devices.

Notes made before you signed in stay on that device until sync is on there too.

10I forgot my sync passcode

Choose Use recovery code instead on the unlock screen and enter the recovery code you saved when you turned sync on. Then set a new passcode.

Your notes are end-to-end encrypted, so we can’t reset or read them. If both the passcode and the recovery code are lost, the synced copy can’t be opened, but notes still on your devices are safe. Turn sync off and on again on a device that has them to start fresh.

11Group notes say “open the invite link”

Group notes are end-to-end encrypted, and the key travels only in the invite link. Open the group’s invite link on each device you use (phone, tablet, computer) to read its notes there. Ask the group owner to send the link again if you lost it.

Creating groups is part of Kavodly Plus; anyone invited can join.

12I see an inappropriate public note

Tap Report under the note. Notes reported by several readers are hidden right away and reviewed by our team.

Reading

13Explain isn’t available or says the limit is reached

Explanations have a daily limit: more when you’re signed in, and many more with Kavodly Plus. The limit resets the next day.

14The text is too small, too bright or hard to read

Tap Aa to change text size, font, line spacing and theme (Paper, White, Sepia, Sage, Rose, Dusk, Night, Black). Lower the story painting behind the text or turn it off there too.

15Put Kavodly on my home screen like an app

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

16Something looks broken or out of date

Pull down to refresh, or close the browser tab and open Kavodly again to get the newest version. If that doesn’t help, write to us with a screenshot.

Avoid clearing website data unless sync is on: notes kept only on this device would be removed.

Privacy

17How do I delete my account and data?

Open 👤 Account → Delete account. It removes your account, sign-in details and city, your synced notes, highlights and bookmarks on our servers, your shared notes and the groups you created, right away and for good. You can copy your notes first, and choose whether to remove them from the device too.

18What does Kavodly know about me?

Only what it needs: your email and name for sign-in, your devices (to let you sign them out), and your city if you allow the readers map, never your exact location or IP address. Synced notes are encrypted on your device; we can’t read them. Read the Privacy Policy.

Still stuck?

Write to us at support@xrainier.com. Tell us your phone or computer, the browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge…), the language you read in, and add a screenshot if you can. We reply as soon as we can.
