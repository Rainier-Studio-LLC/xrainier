Name every peak you can see.

What you get

Your horizon, named

Every peak in view

Real elevation data, Earth curvature and refraction decide what you can actually see — and what hides behind a ridge.

Is it out?

Line of sight plus the National Weather Service’s cloud cover: is Rainier out right now, or hiding?

Panorama or camera

Drag around a painted panorama that follows the time of day, or hold up your phone and see the names over the live view.

Ridge Link

See it? Say hi.

  • Tap “I see it” and join today’s chat for that peak with everyone else who can see it.
  • You choose a trail name — the only name anyone sees. Never your email or account.
  • End-to-end encrypted. Peak chats vanish after 24 hours; private chats open only when the other person accepts.
  • Block and report built in, with real people reviewing reports.
Easy outside

Simple enough for a trailhead. Clever underneath.

  • Start from your location, a favourite viewpoint or any coordinates; add height for a tower or deck.
  • An “In view” list of every peak, with height, distance and direction in feet or metres.
  • Keep a peak log and favourites, synced to your account.
  • Your location stays on your device; only a rounded area is used to fetch terrain.
