How it works

From “I can’t draw” to “look what I made!”

Just say it

Tap the big microphone and ask for a cat, a fire truck or “two unicorns and a rainbow”. Or tap a picture, or type.

Line by line

A pencil draws one simple shape at a time — “a big circle for the head” — or your child traces the dotted line with a finger.

Color, then play

Paint bucket, crayons, stickers and magic colors. Then tails wag, wings flap and wheels spin to the music.

Color and move

Every picture comes alive.

  • Tap to fill, color with crayons (even a rainbow one), add stickers and pick a background: meadow, sea, space, night or snow.
  • Press Make it move!: each picture has its own dance, plus bounce, wiggle, spin and float.
  • Nursery songs and our own tunes play along — and tapping the picture plays notes.
  • Save it to the gallery on your device and come back to it any time.
Made for families

Safe for little artists. Easy for grown-ups.

  • No ads, no chat, no kid accounts. Pictures stay on your device.
  • Voice is off until a grown-up allows it; settings, buying and printing sit behind a grown-up check.
  • English and Chinese, with steps read out loud at a speed you choose.
  • Works on phones, tablets and laptops — even offline for the free pictures.
