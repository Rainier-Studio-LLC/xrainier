Kavodly

Kavodly comes from the Hebrew word kavod: glory, with a sense of weight and honour, as in “the glory of the Lord.” The English ending -ly means “like”, so Kavodly is “glory-like”. “The heavens declare the glory of God.” Psalm 19:1

The Bible, beautifully told.

Read and listen in seven languages. Walk through 42 illustrated stories with their own music. Ask for a gentle explanation, follow every family line, and keep your notes just for you, for everyone, or for your group.

Inside Kavodly

Everything to read, hear and understand

Seven languages

English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, 日本語, Français and Deutsch. Read one, or two side by side, verse by verse.

Read aloud, like a person

A natural, human-sounding voice reads each chapter while the verse lights up. Pick a woman’s or man’s voice, an accent and a speed, and keep going into the next chapter.

Illustrated stories

From Creation to the New Jerusalem: 42 stories, each told in three painted scenes, with the verses from your Bible and a short retelling.

Music for every story

Original, calm music written for each story — strings, harp, flute and choir — playing softly behind the painting as you read.

Gentle explanations

Tap any verse, person or story and choose Kids, Simple or Deep. Clear background and meaning, in your language.

People and relationships

Tap a name in the text to meet the person: their story, parents, children and family tree — and how any two people are related.

Notes, your way

Highlight in four colours, bookmark, and write notes. Keep each note private, share it with everyone, or with your own group.

On all your devices

Sign in with email, Google or Apple. Notes, highlights and bookmarks sync end-to-end encrypted — only you can read them.

A comfortable page

Eight themes from paper to night, six reading fonts, text size and spacing, and a page that turns like real paper under your finger.

Read aloud

Close your eyes and listen

  • Natural voice: a human-sounding reader in English, Chinese, Spanish, French and Japanese.
  • Or any voice on your device — women and men, with accents from many countries.
  • The verse being read is highlighted and follows on screen; the music softly steps back.
  • Keeps reading into the next chapter, like an audiobook.
