Crew stations

Inside the tank

No floating camera and no magic crosshair. You see the battle the way a crew does: through armoured glass, a gun sight and the open hatch.

Station 01 · Driver

Driver's periscope

Steer through the vision blocks with a real instrument panel at your fingertips.

  1. Compass tape: your heading, always in view.
  2. Periscope vision blocks: the only window you get, in three panes.
  3. Speedometer in km/h.
  4. Gear indicator between the dials.
  5. RPM gauge for the engine.
  6. Hull and turret diagram showing where your armour has been hit.
Station 02 · Gunner

Gunner's sight

Range it, lead it, fire. Every shot flies a real ballistic path, and armour angle decides whether it goes through.

  1. Mil scale for holding lead on moving targets.
  2. Stadia rangefinder: estimate range the old-school way.
  3. Laser rangefinder readout in metres, with the sight's zoom level below it.
  4. Loaded round: APFSDS, HEAT or HE.

Period sights

FAQ

Questions

What do I need to play?

A modern browser with WebGL2: current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Nothing to download or install. Open tankwar.xrainier.com and deploy.

Is there an iPhone app?

The TankWar iPhone app is coming soon. Until then, play in the browser.

How do I sign in?

With Google, with Apple, or with a one-time code sent to your email.

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free gets you single player against AI crews and online squads of up to 4 with three starter tanks: the Sherman, T-34-85 and M1A2. TankWar Pro adds all 8 tanks, 32-player battles, Domination and the ranked leaderboard for $14.99 a year.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, anytime. Pro starts with a 7-day free trial.

Is the weather really live?

Yes. Each battlefield uses the current real-world weather at its location, and the sun and moon follow the local time there. Deploy to the Fulda Gap after dark there and you'll be fighting at night.

Is online play fair?

Connections are encrypted and anti-cheat runs on the server, not on your device.
