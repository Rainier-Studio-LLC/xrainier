Live radar

Every plane over your head

Open SkyAbove and see what's flying above you right now, with a card for each flight sorted by distance.

  • Altitude, speed in knots and mph, heading and distance on every card
  • A LIVE badge and a refresh every few seconds
  • Opens instantly with your last scan when you come back
  • Star your favorite flights and airlines
Flight details

Where it's from, where it's going

Tap any flight for the full story: route, aircraft, photos and how the flight is going.

  • Origin and destination, airline and aircraft type
  • Aircraft photos, with a slideshow in Pro
  • Whole-flight altitude and speed history in Pro
  • Find any flight by number, call sign or registration in Pro
  • Share a live link anyone can follow
3D replay

Fly it again, in 3D

Replay a flight on a 2D map or in a full 3D globe, from outside or from the cockpit.

  • 2D and 3D views of the whole flight
  • Cockpit view with a heads-up display
  • 11 detailed aircraft models painted in airline colors
  • Full-fin airline tail art
Showcase

One flight, full screen

Showcase mode turns any screen into a calm flight display: one plane at a time, big and clear, with the time and date.

  • Great on a TV, a spare tablet or a second monitor
  • Cycles through the flights around you
  • Clean screen with no watermark in Pro
Spotters

You saw it. So did they.

Tap I saw it to join the people who spotted the same plane today, and earn badges as you go.

  • Reactions: 👍 nice, 😍 love it, 📸 got a photo, 🔊 loud
  • Nicknames with badges like Heavy hunter, Night owl and Globetrotter
  • Follow other spotters and see what they saw
  • World and local leaderboards, plus rare planes being watched near you
Sky Link

Same flight, far apartNEW

When spotters hundreds of miles apart mark the same flight, SkyAbove links them and shows it on a map.

  • A notice in the app and on the website when it happens
  • A shareable page with the route, the plane live and every spotter's area
  • 200 miles by default, or set your own minimum
  • Areas are approximate: exact locations are never shared
And more

Your sky, your way

Everything you spot and save stays with you across devices when you sign in.

  • Logbook, favorites and today's stats over your house
  • Alerts for rare aircraft, emergency squawks and a flight you're tracking
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code; sync everywhere
  • On iPhone: AR Sky View, Lock Screen Live Activity, widget and night mode
